Help & Guide

Follow these four simple steps to create, organize, and review your flashcards.

4 STEPS
STEP 01

Upload a PDF

Choose a PDF from your lecture materials. TraceCard AI will read through the pages to prepare your flashcards.

STEP 02

Generate flashcards

Pick your preferred card type, how many sets you want, and the number of cards in each set.

STEP 03

Validate each set

Take a look at your flashcards, make changes if needed, and choose which ones to keep or remove.

STEP 04

Review & track

Go through your flashcards and rate how well you remembered each answer. Your next review will be scheduled after 1, 3, or 7 days based on your rating.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does TraceCard AI stand for?

TraceCard AI is a web-based AI flashcard generator designed to help students turn study materials into structured flashcards and track their review schedules more efficiently.

Is TraceCard AI free to use?

Yes, you can use TraceCard AI directly in your browser without paying or installing a separate app.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can use TraceCard AI without creating an account. Your flashcards and review history are saved locally in your browser.

How does TraceCard AI decide when I should review again?

TraceCard AI uses spaced repetition to schedule your next review based on how well you remember the flashcards, helping you focus on topics that need more practice.

Can I trust all the flashcards generated by AI?

AI can occasionally generate inaccurate or incomplete information, so always review and edit your flashcards based on your original study materials before studying.

Why does it sometimes take a moment to load or generate flashcards?

Because TraceCard AI runs on a cloud server and processes AI requests, initial loading or longer study materials may require additional processing time.

Will my flashcards be available on another device?

No. Your flashcards and review history are saved only in the browser and device you use.
